## What it is

**SkyGreeting** (https://skygreeting.com) is a real-time three.js beach-at-dusk fireworks show that became a paid product. People design a show, write words that fireworks spell in the sky, and send it as a link. It plays live in the recipient's browser. The repository started as a sales demo ("Beach Fireworks") and was first built in Codex, then continued in Claude Code from `HANDOFF.md`.

- Free: make, preview and send with the free effects (an ending of about 17 s). Deluxe: **$4.99**, one time: a grand finale (about 48 s in all) with a camera move, a gift-wrapped opening, the sender's name signed in the sky and both initials in a heart, every effect, a private non-expiring link and a video without the watermark. No accounts, no subscription.
- Repo: `rowdybard/Techdemo`. All work is on branch **`ccr-09268299-owgtnw`**. No pull request has been opened (don't open one unless asked). Pushes to this branch go live on skygreeting.com within about 30 seconds.

## How it's built

- **No build step.** Plain ES modules and an import map. three.js 0.186.1 and lil-gui 0.21.0 are copied into `vendor/` (folders named `three-0.186.1`, **hyphen not `@`**: see Gotchas) and served from the site.
- Every module in `src/` exports `create(ctx)` returning `{ update(dt, time), dispose() }`. Update order is `MODULES` in `src/main.js`. Nothing allocates in the render loop. Files stay under about 400 lines.
- Everything is procedural: no textures, models or fonts at runtime.
- **Server:** one Cloudflare Worker (`worker/index.js`, name `techdemo`), static files as assets. Routes: `/api/config`, `/api/checkout`, `/api/share` (free greetings get a short `?g=` link), `/api/stripe-webhook`, `/api/greeting`, `/api/report`, `/api/taken-down`, and `/` when it has `?g=` or `?msg=` (link previews; greeting pages are `noindex`).
- **Showcase greetings** for marketing are Deluxe records kept in code (`worker/showcase.js`), not KV.
- **Storage:** KV namespace `GREETINGS` (id in `wrangler.jsonc`). Paid greetings (`g:<id>`), reports (`r:`), take-downs (`h:`). Each paid greeting is also copied into its Stripe PaymentIntent metadata, and `/api/greeting` rebuilds a missing record from Stripe.
- **Payments:** Stripe Checkout through the REST API. Webhook signature checked with WebCrypto.
- **Config:** `wrangler.jsonc` (price `DELUXE_PRICE_CENTS` = 499, `keep_vars` true). The build command copies every top-level `*.html` (so a new page goes live by existing) plus `robots.txt sitemap.xml favicon.* site.webmanifest icons _headers src vendor` into `.deploy/`. **A new top-level file that isn't a page, or a new folder, that must go live has to be added to that command.**
- **Secrets** live only in the Cloudflare dashboard (Workers → techdemo → Settings → Variables and Secrets): `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, and `RESEND_API_KEY` (emails lost links from /find; not set as of October 7, 2026). Never put `sk_` or `whsec_` keys in chat, files or commits. (A `pk_` publishable key is public.) `GET /api/config` reports whether each is present, never the value.
- **Analytics:** Google Analytics 4, tag `G-BTBT8MCNPJ` (inline in `index.html`). Events from `src/track.js`: `share`, `begin_checkout`, `purchase`, `save_video`. Never sends greeting words, names or the private id.

## What was built (high level)

**Places** (`src/places.js` data, `src/environment.js` host, October 9): the scenery is separate from the show. `config.place.environment` is `'beach'` or `'lake'`; the host module stands where the beach used to be in `MODULES`, builds that place's pieces (beach: ocean, beach, landmarks, lighthouse, walk; lake: land, pines, village, reeds, mirror, lake) and, when the setting changes, disposes them and builds the next place's mid-show. Each place can bring its own camera views (same three keys `sand`/`drone`/`water`, named per place in `places.js`) and a moon. The pieces get their own `AbortSignal` while they're made, so listeners go with them. `ctx.beam` (the lighthouse beam as the smoke reads it) lives in the host so it outlives the beach. A city would be a new list and new pieces, nothing in the show changes. Customize has a Place picker; a greeting's link carries `a:` (the place). **A greeting opens in its place from the first frame:** `main.js` sets `config.place.environment` right after `readLink`, from the link's design (`look.a`) or its occasion's preset (`placeOf` in `occasions.js`); for a short `?g=` link the worker adds `<meta name="sg-occasion">` to the page (`preview()` in `worker/index.js`) so the page knows the occasion with no fetch. Without that a New Year link would build the beach first and swap, with a shader-compile hitch. The audio's sea becomes a faint wind on the lake (a tenth of the level).

**The frozen lake** (the New Year look): `lake.glsl.js` is the shared shape (a wobbling oval, islands, and the terrain `landHeight`, in JS: worked out once when the lake is built, never per frame; only `lakeDistance` is also in GLSL), `land.js` (one polar mesh round the lake, finest at the shoreline, plus small grids for the islands: snow, rock gullies, forest, lit by the moon and the bursts, village glow on the far shelf), `pines.js` (1,600 snow-laden pines, 700 on phones, one instanced draw; each one's ground height is worked out in JS as it's placed, and ones that would land in the water or above the tree line are never made), `reeds.js` (frozen reeds on the near shore, 22 tufts, they fill the lower corners of the shore view), `village.js` (houses with a window grid in the fragment shader, blurring to average light when a window is under a pixel; `uCheer` lights every window at midnight), `ice.glsl.js` + `lake.js` (one quad: black ice with cracks, frost, drifts, and open water that's nearly black with ripples; the open water is a bay at the viewer's feet and a channel round the barge; where the water is, the blotches and the drifts never change, so they're drawn once into a small texture, `uMasks`, when the lake is built), `mirror.js` (the sky, moon, land, pines and village photographed once into a half-float cube map, 512 px a side, 256 on phones; the ice looks it up with a parallax-corrected, roughness-blurred ray, so reflections wobble on water and blur on ice; re-taken at most twice a second when the night's brightness changes; the burst lights are zeroed and everything not marked `userData.mirrored` is hidden during a capture). The sky has a moon (`uMoon`, off on the beach) and the Winter preset turns stars on. Cost (October 9 rework, after the owner saw it lag on their phone): the terrain used to be worked out on the GPU every frame, three times per land vertex for its normals and again for every pine vertex (about 176k on a phone); now it's done once in JS (about 80 ms at load). The land's pixels take 8 to 10 noise lookups (the fine detail and sparkle only within 300 m), the ice skips its cracks past 450 m and its ripples where there's no water, and the clock's quad draws nothing outside the dial before zero and is a third smaller then. Auto quality now measures again when the place changes (it used to measure once, on whatever opened first, so a phone that opened on the beach kept the beach's tier on the lake). Software rendering, same frame at 960×540: 1.73 s before, 1.31 s after (about 25% less; most of what's left is the fireworks pool, shared with the beach).

**The New Year show** (`newyear` in `src/occasions.js`, preset `Winter` on the lake): a calm 8 s (fountains, two gold willows), then `{ countdown: 10 }` hangs the giant clock (`countdown.js`, `clock.glsl.js`: a dial of light 156 m across, a lit arc that shrinks, a sweep hand once a second, seven-segment tube digits; zero at 18 s) with a tick each second (`audio.js`), and at zero the sky goes off. Cues with `zero: s` are timed from the clock's zero: the director (`LEAD` 6.3 s) sends those shells up early so each bursts exactly then, and `fireworks.launchAt(type, x, h, burstAt, palette)` places a shell's launch a fuse earlier (`SHELLS` is 96 records now; `smoke.js` mirrors it). Order: seven shells across the sky, rings, ground shows, the year (`2027`, next year from June on) where the clock was, gold willows at the sides, the words, the name, a free barrage in `classic` colours; Deluxe adds crackle, chrysanthemums, strobes, mines and fans, a 50-shell barrage and a 14-shell crescendo (`barrage()` and `salvo()` in `occasions.js`). The middle of the sky is kept clear while the year and words are up, or they drown in the gold. Free: willow, peony, ring, fountains, candles.

Beach scene (ocean with breaking surf, wet sand, twilight sky, wind), fireworks on a GPU particle pool, 12 classic shells plus 8 Halloween shells and 4 Halloween ground effects, a main barge and two side barges (ground shows, always in the main barge's style), smoke lit by firework light and by the lighthouse beam, Lake Michigan pier and lighthouse with adjustable light, synthesised audio, Customize drawer, greeting builder and occasion endings (birthday, love, congrats, thanks, Halloween), moderation of words, report button and take-downs, Save as video, link previews, terms, privacy and About pages, load screen, parallel shader compile, GA4 and search basics, icons.

**What's paid** (`paidItems` in `src/occasions.js`): one set for every occasion. Free: peony, ring, star, heart, willow, crossette, palm, strobe, double break, fountains, shooters, candles, mines, V fans (Birthday makes crossettes paid, Love rings, Thank you candles), and Halloween's pumpkin, ghost, lanterns. Paid: the showpieces (gold crown, colour-changing dahlia, Saturn, swimming fish, whirlwind, falling leaves), the waterfall, chrysanthemum, crackle, and the other Halloween shells and ground shows. Strobe, double break, mines and fans were made free on October 9 (owner: free up the lesser ones); an effect is free once any occasion lists it as free, and paid if it's in any occasion's Deluxe list and no free one. **A new effect must go in at least one occasion's `deluxe` list**, or it counts as free everywhere. Free links are stripped to free effects when opened (`keepFree`); a paid link only exists as a paid record on the server.

## The ideas pages (search traffic)

Added October 8, 2026 to rank for long-tail searches ("birthday fireworks card with name", "gift for someone who has everything", "New Year's Eve virtual fireworks"...). Ten plain static pages: `/ideas` (the hub) and nine pages, each answering one search with its own message ideas and what's in that occasion's show. Greeting links themselves stay `noindex`.

- **Edit the fragments, not the pages.** `tools/ideas/<slug>.html` is one page: a `key: value` comment block (title, description, h1, lead, image, alt, crumb, occasion, optional text, cta, card, blurb) then the body. `python3 tools/make-ideas.py` wraps them in the shared head, breadcrumb, picture, buttons, "More fireworks ideas" and footer, and writes `<slug>.html` at the repo root. Styles are `src/pages.css`. No script on these pages, one picture each, so they stay fast.
- **To add a page:** write `tools/ideas/<slug>.html`, add the slug to `ORDER` in `tools/make-ideas.py`, add it to `sitemap.xml`, run the generator. `tools/pages-test.mjs` (the second step of `npm run check`) fails if a generated page is stale, a title or description is the wrong length or repeated, a link or picture goes nowhere, a button names an occasion that doesn't exist, an example is over the 24/16 character limits or its stated length ("Seventeen characters") is wrong, a price isn't the product's, or a page isn't in the sitemap or the hub.
- **Buttons:** `/?make=<occasion>` opens the builder on that occasion once the show is up, `&text=HAPPY%20NEW%20YEAR` starts with those words (capped at 24 characters and passed through the word filter). Read in `link.js`, acted on in `builder.js`; they do nothing in embeds, the autoshow or a greeting.
- **Pictures:** `src/og/*.jpg` (1200x630) are real frames of the show, also used as greeting link previews (`worker/index.js` picks `src/og/<occasion>.jpg`). `node tools/make-og.mjs --sheet <name> 7,8,9` renders a contact sheet and saves every frame in `.check/og/`; `--pick <name>=<seconds>` copies one in. It's slow (the headless scene runs at about a fifth of real time), so run several in parallel.
- **New Year's** is now a real occasion (`?make=newyear`); the page's picture `src/og/newyear.jpg` is a frame of the clock over the lake. **Not built yet:** Valentine's and July 4th pages. Search takes weeks to months, so a seasonal page has to be published two to three months ahead: Valentine's by December, July 4th by March.
- **Measuring:** the static pages have no Google Analytics (nothing to consent to). Use Search Console: Performance, by page and by query. With few visitors a day there's little data yet; look at what it already shows before adding pages.
- **Not done on purpose:** thousands of "happy birthday [name]" pages. Google treats mass-produced thin pages as spam.

## Gotchas learned the hard way

- **Analytics records the page title on its own.** The worker rewrites a greeting's `<title>` to "<sender> made you a SkyGreeting", so a sender's name reached Google Analytics (seen in the Realtime "Views by page title" card on October 8, 2026) even though the address was already stripped. The GA config in `index.html` now sets `page_title` explicitly for greetings. Anything else that puts a name or words in the title, the address or an event needs the same care; the privacy page promises Google never sees them. Names recorded before the fix stay in GA until they age out (14 months) or are deleted there.
- **Never use `@` in a served path.** Cloudflare redirects `/vendor/three@x/…` to `%40`, which moved a module's address so the import map no longer redirected `three.core.js` to the minified file; the live site 404'd for a few minutes. After any infrastructure or path change, load the **live** site, not just localhost.
- three.js `compileAsync` skips invisible objects, so the pier compiles when first shown, not at load. `KHR_parallel_shader_compile` is missing in the headless test browser, so that path is gated on the extension and was tested by forcing it.
- `<h1>` and the About text are in `index.html` as screen-reader-only text because the show is a canvas and Stripe and search engines read plain text. Keep terms, privacy, about and the contact email linked from the home page.
- A ground-show spark's brightness is capped in the fragment shader through a flag in the particle colour attribute's spare channel (`pool.groundShow`); `aColor.w` is therefore not free.
- The audio context is created once for the page and never closed (Chromium keeps closed ones alive, which leaked on rebuilds); this deliberately departs from no-leak rule 7.
- **Picking New Year over a remembered design.** Customize remembers each visitor's last settings, so most returning people count as having "a show of their own", and the builder then keeps it and applies only the occasion's words and ending. New Year's frozen lake was missed that way (the owner had to pick it in Customize). `borrowScene`/`returnScene` in `occasions.js` now bring an occasion's setting (place, sky, lake, snow: only for a preset that names a place) over their design without touching their fireworks, and give it back when they pick another occasion; `builder.js` `choose()` does it. Tested through the real chips, fresh and with a remembered neon design. The builder also tells Customize which Style card is in use (`ctx.studio.setStyle`), since the cards only knew about their own taps and showed "Classic" on the lake. Customize remembers the place like the colours, so someone who picked the lake finds it there next visit.
- **Words in the sky** (October 9): the default `look.text` is now empty (it was the demo's "SUNSET COVE", which the home page kept spelling); remembered settings holding that old default are moved to empty (`UPGRADES` in `presets.js`). A random text shell is only picked when there are words (`pickType` in `shells.js`). Customize has a **Words in the sky** box under the fireworks chips: empty is none, anything else is spelled now and then like any other shell (and once, a moment after typing), through the word filter; it hides while a greeting is being made, since the message goes up then, and the builder gives the person's own words back when it closes. Style cards keep the words. The client header demo (`#hero`) still spells the business name when nothing else is set (`link.js`).
- **Place and Style are separate** (October 9): the ❄️ Frozen lake Style card is gone (the Place picker does it). Picking Frozen lake in Place brings the lake's look (`look: 'Winter'` in `places.js`: midnight, moon, falling snow) and going back to Beach gives back the sky and snow it had, or the beach defaults. A Style card picked on the lake changes the fireworks and keeps the lake's sky and snow (`takeScene`/`putScene` in `presets.js`).
- **What makes Deluxe special** (October 9, owner: "make the paid stuff more appealing and feel more special"). Before this a Deluxe ending added about 3 seconds and 8 shells to a 17-second free one, nobody saw it before paying, and the recipient couldn't tell. Built in five steps:
  1. **A grand finale for every occasion** (`grandFinale()` in `occasions.js`): about 22 s timed by when each shell breaks (`zero` cues; with no countdown, zero is the ending's start): an 18-shell build across the sky, 12 mirrored pairs, a breath, a 16-shell crescendo inside 1.5 s with a ground effect layered on it, and a last break of three high shells in gold (Halloween: its own colours). Three ground effects join in. Endings now run free about 17 s with 3 to 5 shells and Deluxe about 41 s with about 65 (New Year: 45 s / 36 against 60 s / 103). **The camera move**: a `{ crane: seconds }` cue (director.js) and `crane.js` lift the camera 9 m and glide it 55 m toward the barge over 7 s, hold, and settle back over 5 s when the cue or the ending ends; camera and orbit target move together, so view limits and dragging still work; it waits while walking and forgets the move if the view is reset.   2. **See it before paying**: while a preview plays, the builder's bar has a **Free · 17 s | ✦ Deluxe · 41 s** switch (lengths from `endingLength()` in `director.js`, the same timing the director uses). Tracked as `deluxe_preview`.
  3. **Gift-wrapped, and signed**: a Deluxe link (not the buyer's own return from checkout) opens on a gold seal with ribbons over the dimmed sky, "Sam sent you / a Deluxe SkyGreeting", **Tap to open** (sound on at 0.6: the tap is the gesture browsers need) or *Open without sound*; nothing plays until then (`wrap()` in `gift.js`, tracked as `open_gift`).
