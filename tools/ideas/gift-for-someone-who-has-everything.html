What they’ll receive

A link to a fireworks greeting with your message and their name in the sky. They open it in their browser and can replay it. You choose the colors and a beach or frozen-lake setting; no app or account is needed. Send it as a small gift on its own, or include the link with another present.

What to put in the sky

You have 24 characters for the message, a second line if you want it, and their name on a line of its own. A few ideas, depending on who it’s for:

  • THANK YOU, MOMWith “FOR EVERYTHING” underneath. For the person who has given you more than you could ever wrap.
  • YOU ARE LOVEDA line for a grandparent, with their name beneath it. Plain, and all it needs to be.
  • YOU DESERVE THISFor the one who never buys anything for themselves.
  • NOW YOU HAVE FIREWORKSFor the one who said “don’t get me anything”. They were right that they didn’t need anything, and now they have fireworks.
  • I MISS YOUFor a friend far away, who’ll be glad to hear it.
  • THANK YOU, KARENFor a boss, a mentor or a neighbor who’s done more than they’ll ever mention.

How it works

  1. Choose the occasion, write your words and add their name.
  2. Choose Free or Deluxe, then use Preview the show to watch that version.
  3. Copy the finished link into your text, email or chat. You choose when to send it.

What it costs

Free includes your words, their name and a selection of fireworks. Deluxe is $4.99, once per greeting, with extra effects, a longer finale, a gift opening and your signature in the sky. A Free link lasts a year; a Deluxe link doesn’t expire. No subscription.

If a paid greeting doesn’t work or you’re not happy with it, email hello@skygreeting.com within 14 days for a full refund. See the terms and refunds.
