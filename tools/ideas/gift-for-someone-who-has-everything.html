Why a fireworks show makes a good gift

It isn’t an object, so there’s nothing to store, return or find a shelf for. It arrives the moment you send it, wherever they are, and it’s unmistakably theirs: their name, your words, and a few moments of sky.

When someone has everything, the gift is really your attention. The things people keep are rarely things. They keep the moments when someone thought of them, took a little trouble, and let them know. A fireworks show is a small way of doing exactly that.

What they’ll receive

A link. When they open it, a short show begins over a beach at dusk, and it ends with your words and their name spelled across the sky in glowing letters. Then a small card tells them who it’s from. They can watch it again, play with the show by tapping the sky to launch fireworks of their own, or make one back for someone else. It plays on a phone or a computer, with no app to install and no account to make.

What to put in the sky

You have 24 characters for the message, a second line if you want it, and their name on a line of its own. A few ideas, depending on who it’s for:

  • THANK YOU, MOMWith “FOR EVERYTHING” underneath. For the person who has given you more than you could ever wrap.
  • YOU ARE LOVEDA line for a grandparent, with their name beneath it. Plain, and all it needs to be.
  • YOU DESERVE THISFor the one who never buys anything for themselves.
  • NOW YOU HAVE FIREWORKSFor the one who said “don’t get me anything”. They were right that they didn’t need anything, and now they have fireworks.
  • I MISS YOUFor a friend far away, who’ll be glad to hear it.
  • THANK YOU, KARENFor a boss, a mentor or a neighbor who’s done more than they’ll ever mention.

Making it feel like a gift

The show is the gift, but a few small choices help it land:

  • Pick the moment. Send it at midnight on their birthday, or on the morning of a day they usually dread.
  • Watch it together. Call them while they open it, or put it on the big screen when you’re in the same room and watch their face.
  • Add something to hold. A handwritten card, or a plate of the cookies they love. It’s a lovely thing to receive alongside a digital gift.
  • Let them keep it. Use Save as video to turn the show into a clip they can keep on their phone.

How it works

  1. Press the button and choose the occasion that fits, or whichever look you like best. You can change the colors, the time of day and the fireworks.
  2. Write your words and their name, then press Preview the show to see exactly what they’ll see.
  3. Send it. On a phone, the share sheet opens so you can text it; on a computer, the link is copied for you to paste.
