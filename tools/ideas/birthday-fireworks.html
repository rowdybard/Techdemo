What they see when they open it

The link opens a short show over a beach at sunset. Ground fireworks start it off, bursts of color follow, and then your message is spelled across the sky in sparks, with their name on a line of its own. A small card at the end says who it’s from, with buttons to watch it again or make one back. It plays in the browser on a phone or a computer, so there’s nothing to install and no account to make.

Birthday messages that fit in the sky

The message holds up to 24 characters, and there’s an optional second line. Short and warm reads best. A few ideas:

  • HAPPY BIRTHDAY SAMTheir name right in the message, or save it for the line of its own.
  • HAPPY 40TH, DADA milestone gets a line to itself. “FROM ALL OF US” works well underneath.
  • 30 AND FABULOUSFor the friend who’d roll their eyes and secretly love it.
  • ANOTHER YEAR OF YOUFor someone you’re glad is around.
  • HAPPY BIRTHDAY NANAThe name you call them, in lights.
  • CHEERS TO YOUWhen you’re not sure what to say, this is enough.

Why it works for a birthday

Most birthday wishes arrive as a thumbs-up or a line in a group chat. A fireworks greeting is something they can watch, replay and show to whoever’s in the room. It suits the friend who lives three time zones away, the grandparent who isn’t on social media but can open a link, and the coworker whose birthday you only remembered this morning.

Make it look like them

Tap Customize before you send and you can change the colors, choose which fireworks go up, pick the ground show, and move the sky anywhere from sunset to midnight. Calm and golden for a grandparent, neon for a teenager, a big finale for a milestone: a few taps and it’s theirs. Press Preview the show to watch it before it goes out.

Free, or Deluxe

Making, previewing and sending is free, with peonies, rings, stars, double breaks, strobes, Roman candles and mines. Deluxe is $4.99, once per greeting. It adds a grand finale that more than doubles the show, with gold crowns, colour-changing dahlias, Saturns, whirlwinds and crossettes while the camera rises toward them, a waterfall of sparks off the barge, plus a gift-wrapped opening, your name signed in the sky and both your initials in a heart, a private link that doesn’t expire and a video without the SkyGreeting mark. There’s no subscription and no account, and the price is shown on the send button before you pay.

Want to keep it?

After the show, Save as video records it as a clip you can keep or post. Free greetings carry a small SkyGreeting mark in the corner; Deluxe ones don’t.
