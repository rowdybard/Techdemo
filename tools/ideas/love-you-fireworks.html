What it looks like

The love greeting is the calm one. A soft sunset, a slow pace, heart-shaped bursts, golden willows that fall like slow light, and fountains along the water. Your message spells out across the sky, then their name on the line beneath it. Nothing rushed.

Words to put in the sky

You get 24 characters for the message and a second line if you want one. Plain and true usually lands best.

  • I LOVE YOUTen characters, and it never gets old.
  • I MISS YOUFor long distance, a work trip, or a hard week.
  • HAPPY ANNIVERSARYPut the year underneath, like “TEN YEARS” or “YEAR ONE”.
  • YOU ARE MY PERSONFor the one who’s on your side.
  • HAPPY VALENTINES DAYTwenty characters, with room for their name below.
  • WILL YOU MARRY MESeventeen characters. See below.

Valentine’s Day, anniversaries and long distance

It’s a good fit when you can’t be there. Send the link first thing in the morning so it’s the first thing they see, or at midnight so it’s waiting when the day begins. If you’re in different time zones, it plays the same wherever they open it.

Asking a question

“WILL YOU MARRY ME” fits in the sky, and it can be the first line of a proposal. A few things worth knowing if you’re planning one. Use Preview the show in the builder to see it yourself, rather than opening the link you’re about to send. Open the link on the biggest screen you have, with them beside you, so you watch the first line appear together. And choose Deluxe if you want a link that’s private and never expires, so it’s still there years later.

Make it softer, or bigger

Customize changes the colors, the pace and the time of day. Pastel for something gentle, gold for something warm, a slower pace for something quiet. Words go through a simple check so that only kind ones go up in the sky.

Free, or Deluxe

Free includes hearts, golden willows, strobes, fountains and V-shaped fans. Deluxe is $4.99, once per greeting, and adds rings, colour-changing dahlias, falling leaves, gold crowns, a waterfall of sparks and a grand finale that more than doubles the show, plus a gift-wrapped opening, your name signed in the sky and both your initials in a heart, a private link that doesn’t expire and a video without the SkyGreeting mark. No subscription, no account.

Keep it

Save as video turns the show into a clip, so they can keep it on their phone and watch it whenever they like.
