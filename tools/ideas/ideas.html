How a SkyGreeting works

  1. Choose an occasion and write your words. A message of up to 24 characters, an optional second line, and their name.
  2. Make it theirs. Customize changes the colors, the fireworks, the ground show, the sky, the wind and the smoke.
  3. Preview, then send. You see exactly what they’ll see. On a phone the share sheet opens; on a computer the link is copied for you.

Questions people ask

Do they need an app or an account?

No. The link opens in the browser on any phone or computer. You don’t need an account either.

What does it cost?

Making, previewing and sending is free. Deluxe is $4.99, once per greeting, and adds every effect and a grand finale, a private link that doesn’t expire, and a video without the SkyGreeting mark. There’s no subscription. About & pricing has the details, including refunds.

How long does a link last?

A free link keeps working for a year. A Deluxe link is private and doesn’t expire.

Can I keep the show?

Yes. Save as video records it as a clip you can keep or post. Free greetings carry a small SkyGreeting mark in the corner; Deluxe ones don’t.

What can I write?

Kind things. Hateful, threatening and sexual words are blocked, and anyone who receives a greeting can report it. Names and words with accents, such as é, ñ and ç, spell out properly.

Just want to watch?

The live show is an endless fireworks display that designs itself as it goes. It starts with the sound off and runs for as long as you leave it on.
