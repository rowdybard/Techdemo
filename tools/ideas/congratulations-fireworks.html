Good moments for it

Anything worth a cheer. A graduation, a new job or a promotion, an engagement, a new baby, a finished marathon, a passed exam, a retirement. The show is built to feel like a celebration: a spray of shooters along the water, crossettes that split into crosses, palms and stars, then your words across the sky.

What to write

Up to 24 characters on the message line, an optional second line, and their name on its own line underneath.

  • CONGRATULATIONSFifteen characters, so it fits on one line with room to spare.
  • YOU DID ITFor the thing they weren’t sure they could do.
  • CONGRATS GRADPut the school or the year on the line below.
  • NEW JOB, NEW YOUSixteen characters, and a good one for a first day.
  • WELCOME BABYAdd the baby’s name as the name line.
  • SHE SAID YESOr “HE SAID YES”, for the people who’ve been waiting to hear.
  • HAPPY RETIREMENTWith “YOU EARNED IT” underneath.

Make it fit the news

The congratulations show starts in bold colors, but Customize lets you change the palette: gold for a graduation, pastel for a new baby, neon for a night out. You can also move the sky from sunset to midnight and turn the pace up for a big finish. Use Preview the show to watch it once before you send.

Send it when it happens

Good news is best answered quickly. The link works in a text, an email or a group chat, and everyone who opens it sees the same show. If you’re celebrating with a group, drop it in the chat and let everyone watch together.

Free, or Deluxe

Free includes crossettes, stars, palms and shooters. Deluxe is $4.99, once per greeting, and adds a grand finale that more than doubles the show, with double breaks, strobes, crackle and mines, plus a gift-wrapped opening, your name signed in the sky and both your initials in a heart, along with a private link that doesn’t expire and a video without the SkyGreeting mark. No subscription and no account.
