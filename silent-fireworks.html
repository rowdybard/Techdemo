Silent fireworks you can watch without the bang

Watch fireworks on your screen with the sound off. You can also make a greeting with your words in the sky and send its link.

Free greeting, or Deluxe for $4.99 once per greeting. What’s included

How to keep it quiet

  • Use the sound button on the show. Set it to Sound off.
  • Opening a Deluxe greeting? Choose Open without sound on its gift screen.
  • Just watching? The endless show starts with sound off.

These settings mute the audio; the fireworks still flash and move on screen.

Choose a slower show

In Customize, try the Calm Look, move Pace toward Calm and Size toward Small. You can choose the colors and setting too. Use Preview the show to check the whole greeting, including its ending.

For a whole room

The endless show changes its fireworks and colors as it runs. If you want to use a TV, try connecting or mirroring a computer first and check the sound on that setup.

Send a greeting

Add a message and their name, choose Free or Deluxe, then preview that version. Copy the link into a text, email or chat and send it yourself. The recipient controls sound when they watch.

Free, or Deluxe

The endless show is free, and Free greetings include your words, their name and a selection of effects. Deluxe is $4.99, once per greeting, with extra effects, a longer finale, a gift opening and your signature in the sky. Both versions let the viewer turn sound off. No subscription or account.
