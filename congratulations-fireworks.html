Make a congratulations fireworks greeting

Free greeting, or Deluxe for $4.99 once per greeting. What’s included

Good moments for it

A graduation, a new job, an engagement, a new baby or a retirement. The congratulations ending puts your words and their name among the fireworks; your choice of colors and setting makes it personal.

What to write

Up to 24 characters on the message line, an optional second line, and their name on its own line underneath.

  • CONGRATULATIONSFifteen characters, so it fits on one line with room to spare.
  • YOU DID ITFor the thing they weren’t sure they could do.
  • CONGRATS GRADPut the school or the year on the line below.
  • NEW JOB, NEW YOUSixteen characters, and a good one for a first day.
  • WELCOME BABYAdd the baby’s name as the name line.
  • SHE SAID YESOr “HE SAID YES”, for the people who’ve been waiting to hear.
  • HAPPY RETIREMENTWith “YOU EARNED IT” underneath.

Make it fit the news

Use Customize to choose colors or a different Look. Put the school, year or new job on the second line. Then choose Free or Deluxe and use Preview the show to check that version.

Send it when it happens

Copy the finished link into a text, email or group chat. You send it yourself, and the recipient opens it in their browser. No account or app is needed.

Free, or Deluxe

Free includes your message, their name and a selection of fireworks. Deluxe is $4.99, once per greeting, with extra effects, a longer finale, a gift opening and your signature in the sky. Free links last a year; Deluxe links don’t expire. No subscription.

A clip to keep

In browsers that support recording, Save as video records a clip. Free videos include a small SkyGreeting mark; paid Deluxe greetings can be saved without it.
