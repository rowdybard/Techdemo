SkyGreeting: send a fireworks show with their name in the sky

Sky Greeting lets you design a fireworks show over a beach at dusk or a frozen lake at midnight, write your own words to spell out in the sky, and send it to someone as a link that plays in their browser. Free to make and send; Deluxe greetings are $4.99 and add a grand finale, a gift-wrapped opening, your name signed in the sky and your initials in a heart. Contact: hello@skygreeting.com.
