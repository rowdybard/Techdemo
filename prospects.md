# Michigan prospect list

First round for the $500 animated header. Built from web searches on October 1, 2026. Before sending, check each business's website and use the contact email or form listed there. Check whether their homepage header is a plain photo: those are the best fits.

**Order of attack:** privately owned businesses first, where the owner decides and can pay $500 without a vote. City and state marinas are listed last. They go through councils and purchasing rules, so they're slow.

**What to put in each mockup:** their name in the Client mockup folder, plus the preset that fits. Use Lake Michigan for anything with a pier or lighthouse nearby, and Gold Willows for restaurants and venues.

## Tracking

Keep a simple log: date sent, which email version, link sent, reply. Follow up once after 5 business days. If one place says yes, ask whether you can show their header to the others nearby. Neighbours in the same town are the easiest second sale.
