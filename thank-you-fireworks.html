What the show looks like

The thank-you ending spells out your message and their name among the fireworks. Choose Gold willows in Customize for a golden show, or use the colors and setting you prefer.

Who to thank

A teacher at the end of term, a host after dinner, a coworker who covered a shift, or a friend who helped. Use the second message line to say what they did.

What to write

Up to 24 characters on the message line, plus an optional second line. Use the second line for the reason; a thank-you lands harder when it’s specific.

  • THANK YOUThe plain version, which is often the best one.
  • THANKS, COACHAdd “FOR THE LIFTS ALL YEAR” on the second line.
  • THANK YOU, MS. LEEWith the year or the class underneath.
  • YOU MADE MY DAYFor the small kindness that stayed with you.
  • IT MEANT A LOTAdd “FOR COVERING MY SHIFT”, or whatever it was.
  • THANKS FOR EVERYTHINGTwenty-one characters, for the person who did everything.

Preview and send

Choose Free or Deluxe, then use Preview the show to check that version. Copy the finished link into a text, email or chat and send it yourself. The recipient opens it in their browser; no account or app is needed.

Free, or Deluxe

Free includes your words, their name and a selection of fireworks. Deluxe is $4.99, once per greeting, with a longer finale, extra effects, a gift opening and your signature in the sky. A Free link lasts a year; a Deluxe link doesn’t expire. No subscription.
