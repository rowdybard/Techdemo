Free greeting, or Deluxe for $4.99 once per greeting. What’s included

What’s in the show

The Halloween ending includes pumpkins, ghosts and your message, with their name beneath it. Choose the Halloween Look in Customize to use its dark sky and spooky effects throughout the show.

Free includes pumpkins, ghosts and floating lanterns. Deluxe is $4.99, once per greeting, with extra effects such as skulls and bats, a longer finale, a gift opening and your signature in the sky. A Free link lasts a year; a Deluxe link doesn’t expire. No subscription.

What to write

The message holds up to 24 characters, with an optional second line.

  • HAPPY HALLOWEENFifteen characters, a good fit for one line.
  • TRICK OR TREATFor the neighbors you trade candy with.
  • STAY SPOOKYEleven characters, and a nice thing to say to a friend.
  • SEE YOU IN THE DARKFor the person you’re meeting later.
  • NO TRICKS, JUST TREATSTwenty-two characters, for the kids in the group chat.
  • BOO!Sometimes that’s all it needs.

Who to send it to

Share it with family who cannot be there for the costumes, your friends’ group chat or a neighbor. Copy the link into your own text or email; the recipient opens it in their browser.

How scary is it?

Free has pumpkins, ghosts and lanterns. Deluxe also includes skulls, bats and lightning. Choose the version you plan to send and preview it first, especially for a younger recipient.

Make it your own

Customize changes the colors, pace and setting. You can add a second message line and choose individual effects. Selecting a Look previews its full design; the Free version may use different effects.
