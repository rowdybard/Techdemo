A Halloween e-card with fireworks

Jack-o’-lanterns, ghosts and floating lanterns burst over a dark beach while your Halloween message spells out in the sky.

What’s in the show

The Halloween greeting trades the sunset for a dark beach and the usual bursts for shapes: jack-o’-lanterns, ghosts and floating lanterns rising off the water. Then your message spells across the sky, and their name beneath it. Everything is drawn in sparks, so the pumpkins glow and then drift away.

Free includes jack-o’-lanterns, ghosts and floating lanterns. Deluxe ($4.99, once per greeting) adds skulls, bats, spider webs, witch’s brew, eyes in the dark, will-o’-the-wisps, bubbling cauldrons, rising wisps, lightning and a grand finale that more than doubles the show, plus a gift-wrapped opening, your name signed in the sky and both your initials in a heart, a private link that doesn’t expire and a video without the SkyGreeting mark.

What to write

The message holds up to 24 characters, with an optional second line.

  • HAPPY HALLOWEENFifteen characters, a good fit for one line.
  • TRICK OR TREATFor the neighbors you trade candy with.
  • STAY SPOOKYEleven characters, and a nice thing to say to a friend.
  • SEE YOU IN THE DARKFor the person you’re meeting later.
  • NO TRICKS, JUST TREATSTwenty-two characters, for the kids in the group chat.
  • BOO!Sometimes that’s all it needs.

Who to send it to

The group chat, the family that can’t be there for the costumes, college friends, the neighbor with the best porch. It’s a good one for grandparents too: they get to see the pumpkins without the walk around the block. If you’re hosting, open the link on a big screen before the doorbell starts.

How scary is it?

More spooky-fun than scary. The free effects are pumpkins, ghosts and lanterns, which are friendly. Deluxe adds skulls, bats and lightning, so preview it first if you’re sending it to someone very young.

Make it your own

Customize lets you change the colors, the pace, the amount of smoke, and you can add the pier and lighthouse in the distance. Press Preview the show to watch it before you send it. Phones open the share sheet; on a computer the link is copied for you.
