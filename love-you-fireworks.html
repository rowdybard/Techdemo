Say I love you with fireworks

Put your words and their name in a fireworks greeting, then send the link to someone you love.

Make an I love you fireworks greeting

Free greeting, or Deluxe for $4.99 once per greeting. What’s included

What it looks like

The love ending includes heart-shaped fireworks and your message, with their name on a separate line. In Customize, choose the Romance look for rose colors and golden willows, or keep a show you have already designed.

Words to put in the sky

You get 24 characters for the message and a second line if you want one. Plain and true usually lands best.

  • I LOVE YOUTen characters, and it never gets old.
  • I MISS YOUFor long distance, a work trip, or a hard week.
  • HAPPY ANNIVERSARYPut the year underneath, like “TEN YEARS” or “YEAR ONE”.
  • YOU ARE MY PERSONFor the one who’s on your side.
  • HAPPY VALENTINES DAYTwenty characters, with room for their name below.
  • WILL YOU MARRY MESeventeen characters. See below.

Valentine’s Day, anniversaries and long distance

Copy the greeting link into a text or email and send it when it suits their day. They can open it later or replay it while you’re on a call together. You send the message yourself; SkyGreeting does not schedule delivery.

Asking a question

“WILL YOU MARRY ME” fits on the message line. Choose Free or Deluxe and use Preview the show to check the words and timing before the moment. If you plan to watch together, try the greeting on that screen first.

Make it softer, or bigger

Customize lets you change the colors, pace and setting. Short messages are easier to read; use the second line for a date or a detail that matters to you both.

Free, or Deluxe

Free includes hearts, your message and their name. Deluxe is $4.99, once per greeting, and adds extra effects, a longer finale, a gift opening, your signature and both initials in a heart. A Free link lasts a year; a Deluxe link doesn’t expire. No subscription or account.

Keep it

In browsers that support recording, Save as video makes a clip to keep. Free videos carry a small SkyGreeting mark; paid Deluxe greetings can be saved without it.
