About SkyGreeting

SkyGreeting · skygreeting.com · hello@skygreeting.com

SkyGreeting is a website where you design a fireworks show over a beach at dusk, write your own message to spell out in the sky, and send it to someone as a link. It plays live in their web browser.

What you get

  • Choose an occasion: birthday, love, congratulations, thank you or Halloween.
  • Write a message, a name and who it's from. They appear spelled out in fireworks.
  • Customize the show: colours, kinds of fireworks, ground fireworks, the sky, wind, smoke, and an optional pier and lighthouse.
  • Send it as a link. The recipient watches it on their phone or computer and can make one back.
  • Optionally save the show as a video file to post on social media.

How it's made

SkyGreeting is designed by Daniel and built with AI coding tools. Everything you see and hear is simulated live in your browser: no stock footage, no video, no AI-generated images.

Pricing

  • Free: making, previewing and sending a greeting with the standard effects costs nothing.
  • Deluxe: $4.99 (USD), one time per greeting. Unlocks the extra effects (such as the Halloween shells and ground shows). You get a private link that doesn't expire and a video without the SkyGreeting mark. The price is shown on the send button before you pay.
  • No subscription and no account. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card details.

Delivery

A Deluxe greeting is a digital item, ready the moment you pay. We show you its link right away and email it to the address you gave at checkout. Lost it? We’ll email it again.

Refunds

If your greeting doesn't work or you're not happy with it, email hello@skygreeting.com within 14 days and we'll refund it in full. More in the terms and refunds.
