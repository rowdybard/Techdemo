How a SkyGreeting works

  1. Choose an occasion and write your words. A message of up to 24 characters, an optional second line, and their name.
  2. Make it theirs. Customize changes the colors, fireworks and setting: a beach or a frozen lake.
  3. Choose Free or Deluxe and preview. The versions can use different effects, so check the one you plan to send.
  4. Share the link. Copy it into your own text, email or chat, or use your browser’s share menu when available.

Questions people ask

Do they need an app or an account?

No. The greeting runs in a compatible phone or computer browser. You don’t need an account either.

What does it cost?

Free includes your message, their name and a selection of fireworks. Deluxe is $4.99, once per greeting, with extra effects, a longer finale, a gift opening and your signature in the sky. No subscription. About & pricing has the full comparison and refund details.

How long does a link last?

A Free greeting link lasts a year. A Deluxe link doesn’t expire. Anyone you share a link with can open it.

Can I keep the show?

In browsers that support recording, Save as video makes a clip to keep. Free videos have a small SkyGreeting mark; paid Deluxe greetings can be saved without it.

What can I write?

Use a message of up to 24 characters per line and a name of up to 16 characters. Accented letters such as é, ñ and ç are supported. The site checks messages for prohibited content, and recipients can report a greeting.

Just want to watch?

The live show is an endless fireworks display that designs itself as it goes. It starts with the sound off and runs for as long as you leave it on.
