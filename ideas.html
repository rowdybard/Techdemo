Fireworks greeting ideas

A SkyGreeting is a short fireworks show with your words spelled in the sky, sent as a link: over a beach at dusk, or for New Year’s, a frozen lake at midnight. Pick an occasion for message ideas.

Pick an occasion

  • BirthdayTheir name and your wishes, spelled out over a beach at dusk.
  • I love youHearts and golden willows over a calm sunset, with your words in the sky.
  • CongratulationsBursts of color with your congratulations spelled in the sky.
  • Thank youGolden willows and your thank-you spelled across the sky.
  • HalloweenPumpkins, ghosts and floating lanterns over a dark beach.
  • New Year’s EveA frozen lake, a clock counting down from ten, and a sky that goes off at zero.
  • Name in the skySpell a name or a few words in sparks across the sky.
  • Silent fireworksA fireworks show for your screen, with the sound off or turned low.
  • For someone who has everythingA fireworks show made just for them. Nothing to wrap, ship or dust.

How a SkyGreeting works

  1. Choose an occasion and write your words. A message of up to 24 characters, an optional second line, and their name.
  2. Make it theirs. Customize changes the colors, the fireworks, the ground show, the sky, the wind and the smoke.
  3. Preview, then send. You see exactly what they’ll see. On a phone the share sheet opens; on a computer the link is copied for you.

Questions people ask

Do they need an app or an account?

No. The link opens in the browser on any phone or computer. You don’t need an account either.

What does it cost?

Making, previewing and sending is free. Deluxe is $4.99, once per greeting, and adds every effect, a grand finale that more than doubles the show, a gift-wrapped opening, your name signed in the sky and both your initials in a heart, a private link that doesn’t expire, and a video without the SkyGreeting mark. There’s no subscription. About & pricing has the details, including refunds.

How long does a link last?

A free link keeps working for a year. A Deluxe link is private and doesn’t expire.

Can I keep the show?

Yes. Save as video records it as a clip you can keep or post. Free greetings carry a small SkyGreeting mark in the corner; Deluxe ones don’t.

What can I write?

Kind things. Hateful, threatening and sexual words are blocked, and anyone who receives a greeting can report it. Names and words with accents, such as é, ñ and ç, spell out properly.

Just want to watch?

The live show is an endless fireworks display that designs itself as it goes. It starts with the sound off and runs for as long as you leave it on.
