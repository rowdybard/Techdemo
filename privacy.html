Privacy policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

The short version: no accounts and no ads. Free greetings aren't stored at all. For paid greetings we keep only what the greeting needs, plus your email to send you the link. We count visits with Google Analytics, which never sees a greeting's words. We never sell anything.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never reach us. Stripe's own privacy policy covers what they do with payment information: stripe.com/privacy.

Who else handles data

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and stores paid greetings and reports. Like any web host, it processes your internet address to deliver the page.
  • Stripe processes payments and sends receipts.
  • Google counts visits (Google Analytics), as described above. We've turned off its advertising features and Google signals, so it isn't used for ads or linked to your Google account. Google's own policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

That's everyone. We don't use advertising networks or sell data. Greetings shown on another website (an embed) don't use Google Analytics at all.

Videos

"Save as video" records the show on your own device. The video is never uploaded to us.

Your choices

  • Ask us what we hold about you, or to delete a paid greeting and its email, by emailing hello@skygreeting.com. We'll reply within 30 days.
  • Clear your browser data to forget your saved design.
  • To keep your visits out of Google Analytics, use Google’s opt-out add-on or a content blocker.

Children

SkyGreeting isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anything from them. If you think a child has bought a greeting, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. See also our terms and refunds.
