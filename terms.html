Terms, privacy and refunds

Last updated October 4, 2026

The short version: make greetings that are kind (or at least affectionate). Don't use SkyGreeting to hurt anyone. We keep only what a greeting needs to work. If a paid greeting doesn't work for you, email us and we'll refund it.

What SkyGreeting is

SkyGreeting lets you design a fireworks show with your own words and send it to someone as a link. The show plays live in their web browser; it isn't a video. How sharp it looks depends on their device, and it needs an internet connection to load.

By making or sending a greeting, you agree to these terms.

What you can't put in a greeting

You're responsible for the words you send. Don't use SkyGreeting to:

  • harass, threaten or bully anyone, or encourage self-harm;
  • send hate speech, slurs, or attacks on people for who they are;
  • send sexual content;
  • impersonate someone, spam, or mislead people;
  • break the law or anyone else's rights.

Mild swearing between friends is fine. We filter some words automatically, and we may take down any greeting that breaks these rules, without a refund.

Reports and take-downs

Anyone who receives a greeting can report it. When several different people report the same greeting, it's taken down automatically, and we review reports by hand. If you think we took yours down by mistake, email us.

Payments and refunds

  • Making and previewing a greeting is free, and so is sending the free version. Deluxe greetings cost the price shown on the send button before you pay.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
  • A paid greeting is a digital item that's ready as soon as you pay, with a private link that doesn't expire.
  • If your paid greeting doesn't work, or you're unhappy with it, email us within 14 days and we'll refund it.

Privacy

Free greetings aren't stored by us at all; paid greetings keep only what they need, plus your email to send you the link. No ads and no accounts; we count visits with Google Analytics, which never sees a greeting's words. The details are in our privacy policy.

Changes

We may update these terms as SkyGreeting grows. The date at the top shows when they last changed.
